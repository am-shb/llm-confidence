## Section 8 -- contrasts

### C1 -- J vs F1-V, J vs F2-V, raw

Diff = (arm's raw Brier) - (J's raw Brier); negative means the arm has the LOWER, better Brier, i.e. J loses.

- J vs F1-V: raw Brier diff (F1-V - J) = -0.0441 [-0.0619, -0.0267] -- F1-V has the lower/better raw Brier (J loses). Excludes zero; margin exceeds the PC2 floor (0.0005).
- J vs F2-V: raw Brier diff (F2-V - J) = -0.0457 [-0.0623, -0.0306] -- F2-V has the lower/better raw Brier (J loses). Excludes zero; margin exceeds the PC2 floor (0.0005).

### C2 -- same after scaling, plus calibration gaps

- J vs F1-V: scaled Brier diff (F1-V - J) = -0.0267 [-0.0396, -0.0129] -- F1-V has the lower/better scaled Brier (J loses). Excludes zero; margin exceeds the PC2 floor (0.0005). Calibration-gap diff (gap_F1-V - gap_J) = -0.0174 [-0.0270, -0.0085] (positive means F1-V lost more to miscalibration than J did).
- J vs F2-V: scaled Brier diff (F2-V - J) = -0.0236 [-0.0354, -0.0121] -- F2-V has the lower/better scaled Brier (J loses). Excludes zero; margin exceeds the PC2 floor (0.0005). Calibration-gap diff (gap_F2-V - gap_J) = -0.0222 [-0.0299, -0.0148] (positive means F2-V lost more to miscalibration than J did).

### C3 -- Q-L vs Q-V (mechanism, model held fixed)

Raw Brier diff (Q-V - Q-L) = -0.0621 [-0.0796, -0.0443]: Q-V has the lower (better) raw Brier than Q-L by 0.0621, i.e. the verbalized mechanism (Q-V) wins this matched-model contrast. Excludes zero; margin exceeds the PC2 floor (0.0005).

### C4 -- J vs Q-L, raw (asymmetric)

Raw Brier diff (Q-L - J) = 0.1727 [0.1468, 0.1963]. margin exceeds the PC2 floor (0.0005).

**J beats Q-L on raw Brier: this is a WEAK result.** Q-L is a 27B open-weights model, so a J win here reintroduces the model-quality confound -- it does not establish that J's mechanism, specifically, is what wins.

### Attribution: how much of C1 does C3 explain?

J's advantage over F1-V (F1-V brier - J brier) = -0.0441; over F2-V = -0.0457. Conservative C1 margin (the smaller of the two) = -0.0457. C3's mechanism effect (brier(Q-V) - brier(Q-L); positive favours the native/logprob mechanism) = -0.0621.

**Attribution does not apply: J does not beat both frontier baselines on raw Brier.** There is no positive C1 margin for a mechanism to explain -- see the "better out of the box" verdict above, which already fails on this basis. Separately, and without implying anything about J: C3 shows the verbalized mechanism (Q-V) beating the native/logprob one (Q-L) by 0.0621 on the one model where both are measured.

## Section 8 decision rules -- verdicts

**"Better out of the box than typical LLM usage"** -- holds only if J beats BOTH F1-V and F2-V on raw Brier, intervals excluding zero, margins above the PC2 floor.

**Verdict: DOES NOT HOLD.** F1-V check passed=False, F2-V check passed=False.

**"Calibrated"** -- holds only if J's raw ECE is below 0.05 AND its calibration-gap interval includes values near zero. Reported for both of J's ECE bases.

- max(probabilities) basis: raw ECE = 0.1296 (>= 0.05, FAILS the cutoff).
- confidence-field basis: raw ECE = 0.1089 (>= 0.05, FAILS the cutoff). No calibration-gap variant exists for this basis (scalar, cannot be temperature-scaled).
**Verdict on max(probabilities): DOES NOT HOLD** (ECE cutoff alone determines it here; the calibration-gap leg is moot once the ECE cutoff already fails, and is reported above under C2 regardless).

**A, P and P\* are excluded from every contrast and every verdict above.** They appear only in the section 7 table. P\* is labelled an oracle throughout.
